University of Moratuwa

Excellence in Engineering, Technology, and Innovation

The University of Moratuwa is one of Sri Lanka's leading universities, well known for engineering, architecture, information technology, Medical and business education. It is located in Moratuwa, Sri Lanka.

  • Faculty of Information Technology

  • Faculty of Business

Why Choose UoM?

The university offers high-quality education, modern research facilities, experienced academic staff, and strong industry collaborations that prepare students for successful careers.
